The story

Arcade Video game published 42 years ago: Hyper Sports (c) 1984 Konami Export release. Game developed in Japan. See the original for more information; "Hyper Olympic '84 [Model GX330]".

TECHNICAL

Game ID: GX330

TRIVIA

Hyper Sports was released in July 1984 by Centuri in the US, under license from Konami. Kelly Kobashigawa holds the official record for this game with 538340 points.
