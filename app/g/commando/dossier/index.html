The story

Arcade Video game published 41 years ago: Commando (c) 1985 Data East USA, Inc. North American release. Game developed in Japan. See the original for more information; "Senjou no Ookami".

TRIVIA

Commando was released by Data East USA, under license by Capcom, in July 1985 in the USA. It was the first (and and only) game that Capcom licensed to Data East USA. Tim Balderramos holds the official record for this game with 10051200 points on July 5, 1986.
