## The story

Arcade Video game published 43 years ago:

Track & Field (c) 1983 Konami.

Export release. Game developed in Japan. See the original for more information; "Hyper Olympic [Model GX361]".

### Technical
Game ID : GX361

### Trivia
Track & Field was released in October 1983 by Konami.

A Bootleg of this game called "Atlant Olimpic" was released in Italy in 1996, thirteen years after the game first appeared. 

A Track & Field unit appears in the 1985 movie, 'The Goonies'.
