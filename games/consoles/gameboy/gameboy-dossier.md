### Cartridges

The machine itself needs no ROMs — all software comes on cartridges. 1,743 cartridges are catalogued from the MAME `gameboy` software list; 7 PCB types are currently supported (`rom`, `rom_mbc1`, `rom_mbc2`, `rom_mbc3`, `rom_mbc5`, `rom_huc1`, `rom_mmm01`). Drop your own legally-dumped cart files onto the console page to play.
