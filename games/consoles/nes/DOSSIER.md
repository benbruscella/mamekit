### Cartridges

The machine itself needs no ROMs — all software comes on cartridges. 4,545 cartridges are catalogued from the MAME `nes` software list; the cartridge slot declares 402 boards, and every one that compiles from MAME source is playable. Drop your own legally-dumped cart files onto the console page to play.
